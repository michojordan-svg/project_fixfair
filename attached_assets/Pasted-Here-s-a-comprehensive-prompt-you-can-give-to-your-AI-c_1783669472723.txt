Your task is NOT to make small fixes. Your mission is to perform a complete production-ready audit of the entire application and deliver a stable, fully functional app.

* Ensure every screen works.

* Ensure audio recording works.

## IMPORTANT

Do NOT claim that everything is working unless it has actually been verified through successful builds and tests.

If any feature cannot be verified because of missing credentials, APIs, backend services, physical device requirements (such as camera or microphone), or external dependencies, clearly state what could not be tested and why.

Only report a feature as "working" if you have concrete evidence from successful execution or automated tests. Otherwise, report it as "fixed but not fully verified" or "requires manual testing."

The goal is a production-ready application with no known critical bugs, no placeholder functionality, stable authentication, and reliable media recording.

One important note: no AI agent can honestly guarantee that **everything** in an app works unless it can actually run the app, build it successfully, execute tests, and in some cases test on a real device (especially for camera, microphone, and permissions). A trustworthy agent should distinguish between features it has verified and those that still require manual testing. This makes the final report much more reliable.
